Custom web application development

I build web applications around the way your team actually works: internal tools, CRMs, client portals, prospecting platforms. Today I build them at SIMTIS, for teams in Morocco and abroad.

Problems I solve

  • sales teams that track prospects in spreadsheets or inboxes;
  • companies that need an internal tool nothing on the market provides;
  • groups with several stores or business units that want one shared view;
  • projects that must work in several languages, Arabic included.

What I build

  • web applications and business platforms in React, Node.js and TypeScript;
  • CRMs and prospecting tools, with a pipeline, follow-ups and reports;
  • integration with your existing data and tools: APIs, files, ERPs, external services;
  • automation of your team’s repetitive tasks;
  • interfaces in French, English and Arabic, right to left where needed.

How I work

  1. Understanding your work

    I first look at how your team works today and what slows it down. The tool follows that work, not the other way round.

  2. A clear scope

    I write the need down, split into stages, with a schedule.

  3. Regular deliveries

    I build in short stages with a demo each time, so we can adjust before it is too late.

  4. Testing, go-live and training

    The tool is tested before it goes live. I write the guides and train the people who will use it.

  5. Follow-up after delivery

    After delivery, I stay on the follow-up: maintenance, fixes and new features.
