AI automation and team training in Morocco

I add AI where it really saves time: writing, sorting, qualifying leads, producing visuals. And I train teams in Casablanca and across Morocco to use it every day, while they stay in charge of every decision.

Problems I solve

  • teams that spend hours on repetitive writing or sorting;
  • sales staff who have to qualify a lot of prospects;
  • brands that need many product visuals;
  • companies that want to adopt generative AI without getting lost in the tools.

Three service levels

    AI Integration

    Add an AI feature to an application your team already uses.

    • Connect AI APIs to the relevant data in your application.
    • Assist with writing, generate visuals or qualify leads within existing screens.
    • Bring results into the workflow, with human review.

    AI Automation

    Automate a business process by connecting its steps, data and tools.

    • Workflows with Make and Claude to write, sort, enrich or qualify.
    • Coordinate tasks and pass results between tools.
    • Approval checkpoints, activity tracking and error handling.

    AI Product Development

    Design and build a complete application with AI at the core of its functionality.

    • Scope, interface, business logic, data and AI model integration.
    • Users, permissions, quotas and review workflows tailored to the project.
    • Testing, launch and follow-up: for example, a visual production tool like Simtis Studio or a platform like B2B Lead Intelligence.

    Training and adoption at every level

    I help teams get started through department workshops, custom Claude Skills and practical guides. The goal is an everyday tool people understand and can use while staying in control of decisions.

    What you receive

    Deliverables depend on the selected service and are agreed before development. Maintenance and further improvements are scoped separately.

    • AI Integration: an AI feature added to your application, with defined inputs, outputs and review steps.
    • AI Automation: a configured and tested workflow with triggers, connected tools and approval checkpoints.
    • AI Product Development: a complete application with interface, data, user access and AI features within the agreed scope.
    • For the selected service: documentation, user training, usage guidelines and cost monitoring guidance.

    The first conversation

    We review your current workflow, users, tools and constraints, then identify the appropriate next step: scoping, a prototype or a delivery proposal.

    How I work

    1. Finding what is worth it

      With your team, I spot the tasks where AI really saves time, and the ones where it adds nothing.

    2. Validate a first version

      Depending on the scope, I build an initial integration, workflow or application prototype to validate its usefulness with your team.

    3. Guardrails

      Quotas, human approval, an activity log: the AI suggests, someone on the team decides.

    4. Training and documentation

      I train the people who will use the tool and write simple guides.

    5. Improving

      We fine-tune the settings based on the team’s feedback.
