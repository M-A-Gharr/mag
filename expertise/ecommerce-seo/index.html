E-commerce and SEO: PrestaShop, WordPress and Shopify

I build, redesign and improve online stores that load fast, get found on Google and stay easy to run day to day. Mostly on PrestaShop, but also on WordPress and Shopify, in French, English and Arabic.

Problems I solve

  • brands whose online store is hard to find on Google;
  • retailers with physical stores who want their stock connected to their website;
  • stores that want to change platform without losing their customers or their rankings;
  • websites that must work in several languages, Arabic included.

What I build

  • new PrestaShop, WordPress and Shopify stores, and redesigns;
  • custom PrestaShop and WordPress modules;
  • platform migrations, with customers, orders and redirects;
  • SEO, including multilingual websites;
  • theme customisation and a better shopping experience;
  • day-to-day maintenance, updates and security.

How I work

  1. An audit first

    I review the store, its rankings, its data and its plugins before suggesting anything.

  2. A staged plan

    I set the priorities, split into stages, with a schedule.

  3. Building without breaking

    I work on a copy of the store and test every change before it goes live.

  4. Go-live and follow-up

    After go-live, I keep an eye on rankings and errors.

  5. Maintenance

    I then look after the store’s maintenance, security and new features.
