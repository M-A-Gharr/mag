Medical practice management: records, appointments and invoicing

In late 2020 and early 2021, I built a C# application from scratch to digitise client and insurance records and support the medical practice’s daily administration, from appointments to invoicing.

Project technical overview

Technology stack

C#, OCR

Architecture and workflows

C# application combining records, role-based permissions, appointments and billing for a medical practice.

Integrations and data

OCR-assisted digitisation, Excel imports, accounting exports and invoice delivery by email.

My role and responsibilities

Developing practice management features: records, booking, permissions and billing, within the 2020–2021 project.

The challenge

The practice needed to transfer handwritten and Excel records into a database, give each staff role appropriate permissions, and coordinate appointments and invoices.

What I built

  1. Digitising records

    An OCR-assisted workflow helped doctors or secretaries transfer client and insurance information from handwritten books into the new database. Excel imports and accounting exports supported the exchange of data.

  2. Organising roles and appointments

    The application assigned permissions to each staff role. When a client called to book, the assistant used a dedicated booking screen to check availability and assign the appointment.

  3. Preparing and sending invoices

    Invoices included deductions for insurance and supplementary coverage. The application also allowed invoices to be sent directly to clients by email.

The application combined record digitisation, role-based access, appointment scheduling, invoicing and accounting exchanges. I handed the code to the owner; its current status is unknown.
