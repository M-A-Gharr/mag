The need

The manufacturer offers nonwoven rolls and finished products for several applications. Trade buyers need to identify a suitable range, understand customisation options and contact the team with a specific requirement.

Making the industrial offering understandable

I prioritised custom bags and PP spunbond rolls on the homepage. Agricultural, home and hygiene applications complete the navigation by range.

Manufacturing know-how and quality processes provide context alongside the products before buyers get in touch.

A catalogue leading to a quote

Visitors move from a range to a product sheet and then to a quote request. Depending on the product, colour, size and finishing options help prepare the conversation.

The website lets buyers explore products and describe their needs. Quantities, specifications and terms are then discussed with the sales team.

Adapting to markets and devices

The website is available in French, English and Spanish. Navigation, catalogue browsing and quote entry points are designed for desktop and mobile.

The website and sales follow-up

The website presents the offering and collects enquiries. A separate prospecting platform organises portfolios, qualification and follow-ups; its interface includes a website enquiry inbox.

The CRM case study covers that separate scope. This study distinguishes the public website journey from the sales team’s processing of enquiries.
