A reusable application foundation for public share offerings

For my 2020 final-year project at Eurafric Maroc, I worked on a Java/JEE application foundation designed for reuse across banking agencies. Its client onboarding workflow concerned participants in public offerings of shares (OPV).

The challenge

The project needed a reusable foundation for banking agencies, including authentication, onboarding clients participating in public offerings of shares (OPV), and generating PDF documents.

The OPV workflows also needed to respect permissions, the purchase price set by the company and limits on the number of shares available to each client.

What I built

My contribution focused on the authentication component and the application foundation, using Java/JEE and REST APIs. The application also supported onboarding clients participating in public share offerings (OPV) and generating PDF documents.

The application also supported permissions and purchase restrictions, including the price set by the company and limits on the number of shares each client could acquire.

  • Permissions and purchase restrictions for OPV workflows
  • Share purchase price set by the company
  • Limits on the number of shares each client can acquire
